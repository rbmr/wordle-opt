Wordle Policy Visualizer

Any wordle strategy can be visualized as a tree. Every node is a guess, and every edge is a response, where the leaves of the tree (with no outgoing edges) correspond to a win. You can load one of the example policy trees below, or load one of your own, explore it, and use it to play a game of Wordle.
